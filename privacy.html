Alkyon · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Applies to the Alkyon web app (alkyonfishing.com, previously dinospapani.github.io/Alkyon), the Alkyon apps for Android and for iPhone/iPad, and the Alkyon apps for Apple Watch, Wear OS and Garmin watches.

In short: Alkyon only stores what you log, so it can show it back to you and sync it between your devices. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. Other people see something of yours only when you choose it: friends you add, community posts you publish, a safety contact you set, or an SOS you send.

What is collected

  • Your account: email address and an optional display name, if you create an account. If you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Apple, Alkyon receives only your name and email address (with Apple, possibly a private relay address), and uses them only to create and identify your account. No contacts, files or other data from those accounts.
  • What you log: catches, lost fish, trips (including the GPS track of a trip), fishing spots, gear and tackle, boat details, notes and photos, with the time and the GPS position you saved with them. If you are signed in, they are synced to your account; photos are kept in private storage that only you (and friends you share with, see below) can open. Cloud backups you make are stored in your own private folder.
  • Location during a trip: when you start a trip, the app records your GPS track on the phone, also while the screen is locked, until you end the trip. On Android this runs as a foreground service with a visible notification; on iPhone the location indicator is shown. The app does not use your location in the background when no trip is running. The track is saved with the trip.
  • Safety features (optional): the safety contact you enter (name, phone, email) and the Telegram link you set up, and your trip plan (return time, route). If you turn on alerts to your safety contact for a trip, your phone also sends your position, battery level and time to the server about every 3 minutes while the trip is active, so the server can tell your contact if you are overdue or stop responding. Automatic alerts to your contact are sent through Telegram. Messages you choose to send yourself by SMS or WhatsApp go from your own phone.
  • SOS (when you press it): your name and position are shown to your friends and to Alkyon users whose position in the last hour is within about 20 nautical miles, for up to 6 hours or until you mark yourself safe, and nearby boats and friends get a notification.
  • Live trip (optional): if you turn it on when you start a trip, your position and number of fish are sent about every 2 minutes and shown to friends who share with you. Your last live position stays visible to them until your next live trip replaces it, or for up to 90 days.
  • Watches: when you log a catch on the Apple Watch, Wear OS or Garmin watch, it sends the time, the GPS position and the type of entry (fish / lost / toadfish), together with your link code, so the catch can be added to your logbook. To show the forecast, the watch sends its approximate position. Barometer readings stay on the watch.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn them on, a push subscription and the places you chose to watch, so the server can send forecast and safety alerts. Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) on Android, Apple Push Notification service on iPhone, and the browser's push service on the web.
  • Sea reports and port notes (optional): a sea report you send (position, sea state, wind, short note) is shown to signed-in users for 6 hours, without your name, and is deleted after 7 days. A port or ramp note you send is shown with your name after it is approved.
  • Community (optional): posts you publish (species, weight, length, bait, technique, day, the weather if you include it, and the photo), comments, likes, and your community name and picture are public: anyone can see them, even without an account. Posts do not include your position. Photos are re-encoded before upload, which removes the location data stored inside them.
  • Help the forecast (optional, off unless you agree): if you turn on Profile › Settings › Help the forecast, for each catch, lost fish and trip the app sends: species, time, the weather at that time (wind, waves, pressure, cloud, air and water temperature, tide), technique, bait, the lure you picked from your tackle (its colour and size), depth, the target species of a trip (if you pick one), the species name you typed, your «How was the fishing?» rating of a trip, how many fish, and an area rounded to about 10 km. The server adds the hourly weather for those hours from Open-Meteo's public history. It also adds the satellite water chlorophyll for that day and area from Copernicus Marine (only the rounded area and the date are used). Never your name, exact spot, notes or photos. Only the server reads these rows, to build anonymous totals of when fish bite (shown only when at least 20 catches exist), and a weekly list of species being caught in each area of about 100 km (shown, and notified to people with a saved spot there, only when at least 3 anglers reported them). From your own rows it also builds personal findings («For you») that only you can read, and combined findings for you and the friends who already share their catches with you («Crew»), and may send you a notification when tomorrow's forecast matches the conditions you catch most in. Turning it off deletes all of your rows from the server.
  • Fish identification with AI (optional): only if you enter your own Anthropic API key. The photo is then sent directly from your device to Anthropic to suggest the species. Alkyon's server does not receive it, and the key stays on your device.
  • Feedback (optional): when you use Share feedback, your message, the email you choose to add, the app version, the page and the phone type. If you are signed in, it is linked to your account so it is deleted with it.
  • Launch list (optional, website): if you leave your email on alkyonfishing.com to be told when Alkyon launches, we store only that email, the page language and the date. It is used only to send the launch announcement, is never shared or sold, and is deleted after the announcement is sent. To be removed sooner, email alkyonfishingapp@gmail.com.
  • Error reports: short technical error messages (app version, page, error text). These do not include your position or your catches. If you are signed in, they are linked to your account so they are deleted with it.

Permissions on the phone

Location (forecast for where you are, the map, catches and trips), camera (photos of a catch, only when you take one), photos (to pick or save a photo), microphone (voice notes and voice logging, only while you record), and on iPhone the barometer (air pressure saved with your trips and catches). Voice recordings stay on your device. Turning speech into text uses your phone's speech recognition service (Google or Apple). Each permission is asked for when you first use the feature, and you can refuse or remove it in the phone settings.

How it is used

Only to provide the features of the app: your logbook, sync between your devices and your watch, forecasts, alerts, safety messages, SOS, and sharing with the people you choose.

Friends

You connect with a friend by exchanging a one-time code. Connecting turns sharing on in both directions. A friend who shares with you can see your catches, spots and trips that are not marked private, including the exact position, the GPS track, notes and photos, and your live position when you turn it on for a trip. You can stop sharing with any friend at any time in Profile › Friends, and mark any catch, spot or trip as private.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in the app's database and file storage (Supabase) and on your own device. The app itself is served by Cloudflare. Notifications go through Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), Apple (Apple Push Notification service) or your browser's push service; safety alerts through Telegram. Weather, sea, seabed and map data are requested from Open-Meteo, Copernicus Marine, EMODnet, EUMETSAT, RainViewer and map tile services (such as Esri, OpenStreetMap and OpenSeaMap) using a position or map area only; no account details are sent with those requests, but like any website they see your device's IP address.

How long it is kept

  • What you log and your account: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Completed safety trips (plan, last position, battery) and live trip positions: 90 days.
  • Notification history and error reports: 30 days. Feedback messages: until we delete them, and always together with your account.
  • Sea reports: 7 days.

Your choices

  • You can use most of the app without an account. Your data then stays only on your device.
  • You can delete any catch, spot or trip in the app at any time, and mark any of them as private.
  • You can stop sharing with a friend, and stop taking part in Help the forecast, at any time; your Help the forecast rows are then deleted from the server.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from Profile › Account, or ask us by email (see alkyonfishing.com/delete-account). Requests by email are completed within 30 days.
  • To stop a watch from sending data, remove the link code from the watch app, or create a new code in the watch settings in Profile. The old code then stops working.

Άδειες στο κινητό

Θέση (πρόγνωση εκεί που είσαι, χάρτης, ψάρια και έξοδοι), κάμερα (φωτογραφία ψαριού, μόνο όταν τραβάς), φωτογραφίες (για να διαλέξεις ή να αποθηκεύσεις φωτογραφία), μικρόφωνο (φωνητικές σημειώσεις και καταγραφή με φωνή, μόνο όσο ηχογραφείς), και στο iPhone το βαρόμετρο (η πίεση αποθηκεύεται με τις εξόδους και τα ψάρια). Οι ηχογραφήσεις μένουν στη συσκευή σου. Η μετατροπή φωνής σε κείμενο γίνεται από την υπηρεσία αναγνώρισης φωνής του κινητού σου (Google ή Apple). Κάθε άδεια ζητείται την πρώτη φορά που χρησιμοποιείς τη λειτουργία, και μπορείς να την αρνηθείς ή να την αφαιρέσεις από τις ρυθμίσεις του κινητού.

Μόνο για τις λειτουργίες της εφαρμογής: ημερολόγιο, συγχρονισμός ανάμεσα στις συσκευές και στο ρολόι σου, πρόγνωση, ειδοποιήσεις, μηνύματα ασφαλείας, SOS, και κοινή χρήση με όποιους επιλέγεις.

Φίλοι

Συνδέεσαι με έναν φίλο ανταλλάσσοντας έναν κωδικό μιας χρήσης. Με τη σύνδεση η κοινή χρήση ανοίγει και από τις δύο μεριές. Ένας φίλος που μοιράζεται μαζί σου βλέπει τα ψάρια, τα σημεία και τις εξόδους σου που δεν είναι ιδιωτικά, με την ακριβή θέση, τη διαδρομή GPS, τις σημειώσεις και τις φωτογραφίες, και τη ζωντανή θέση σου όταν την ανοίγεις σε μια έξοδο. Μπορείς να σταματήσεις την κοινή χρήση με οποιονδήποτε φίλο, όποτε θέλεις, στο Προφίλ › Φίλοι, και να σημειώσεις οποιοδήποτε ψάρι, σημείο ή έξοδο ως ιδιωτικό.

Πού αποθηκεύονται και ποιοι τα επεξεργάζονται

Τα δεδομένα αποθηκεύονται στη βάση και στον χώρο αρχείων της εφαρμογής (Supabase) και στη δική σου συσκευή. Η εφαρμογή φιλοξενείται στο Cloudflare. Οι ειδοποιήσεις περνούν από τη Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), την Apple (Apple Push Notification service) ή την υπηρεσία push του browser σου· οι ειδοποιήσεις ασφαλείας από το Telegram. Τα δεδομένα καιρού, θάλασσας, βυθού και χάρτη ζητούνται από Open-Meteo, Copernicus Marine, EMODnet, EUMETSAT, RainViewer και υπηρεσίες χαρτών (όπως Esri, OpenStreetMap και OpenSeaMap) μόνο με μια θέση ή περιοχή χάρτη· δεν στέλνονται στοιχεία λογαριασμού, αλλά, όπως κάθε ιστότοπος, βλέπουν τη διεύθυνση IP της συσκευής σου.
